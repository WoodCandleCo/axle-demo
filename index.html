At launch

Everything you see on this screen works on day one.

  • LiveQueue chips are buttons — click one and you land in the queue where that work gets cleared; a queue at zero drops off the strip entirely
  • NoteDay one the strip carries punch corrections, open defects, and expiring documents. Deep-check reviews, purchase requests, low stock, tools overdue, incidents, and urgent acks appear as their phase-2 modules land
  • LiveSix stat tiles: on the clock, vehicles active, utilization, cost/mile, open defects, PM compliance
  • NoteUtilization and PM compliance stay thin until a few weeks of trips and services accumulate
  • LiveUrgent broadcast banner with acknowledgement tracking
  • LiveNeeds-attention feed — defects, expiring licenses, fuel exceptions, weather
  • NoteDay one the only inspection rule is the deterministic one: marked OK while a defect is open. Pattern-based anomalies wait for history
  • LiveCost-per-mile and spend charts

This screen is the one phase-1 page that starts partly dark — live positions need GPS, which ships in phase 2.

  • LiveThe trips table works day one: who is out, in which truck, with which riders, towing what, and when their trip started and ended
  • LiveSearch by person or unit, and click a driver to see their day — punch, departure, each site they checked into

  • NoteThe map itself, live speed, and ETAs turn on in phase 2 when phone GPS starts reporting during trips. Nothing goes in the vehicles either way

Open question
  • ?Should a map dot represent the person or the vehicle? A truck with three crew aboard is one dot today. Person-dots answer “where is Diego” directly but stack on top of each other in a shared truck; vehicle-dots stay clean but need the roster underneath to answer for a rider. A third option: vehicle dots, with the driver’s name as the label and riders listed on click.
