What phase 1 needs

Every account, purchase, decision, and access grant required to launch Axle's phase 1 — the time clock, tags and trips, inspections, the asset register, fuel import, saved sites, and reports. Ordered by when it has to exist, not by category.
Updated Aug 4: the phase-1 crew app ships as a web app, so both app-store accounts and both annual platform deadlines leave the launch path entirely — they become phase-2 prerequisites. Wood Candle Co. holds the developer accounts and already has a D-U-N-S number, so that wait is gone too. What used to be the two long poles is now a two-line to-do for later.

1 · Store accounts — no longer a launch blocker

The crew app is a web app in phase 1: crew open it in a browser, add it to their home screen, and tag taps open it directly — no install, no review, no store. These accounts are only needed when the phase-2 native app arrives for background GPS and push. Wood Candle Co. holds them and already has a D-U-N-S number, so enrollment is a form, not a wait. Worth doing a few weeks before phase 2 starts, since Apple verification takes days.
