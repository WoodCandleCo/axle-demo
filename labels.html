Label face — full-color candidates

Fixed physics on every option: 2″ × 4″ die-cut (J is the same label applied vertically), 1.1″ tap target marking the coin, scannable QR, asset name + unit · placement + purpose. Background is always the full action color; darker shades of the SAME family mark zones (bands, strips, panels) — never a second hue. Shown at 90 px = 1″, blue + yellow (the contrast stress test).
